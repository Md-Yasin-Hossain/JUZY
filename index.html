About JUZY

A flavor system that lives in the cap

JUZY is a flavor-storing bottle cap that holds a sealed, pre-measured portion of fruit-flavor powder. Press the top of the cap and the compartment opens, releasing the powder into your water. A few seconds of shaking dissolves it, and your drink is ready.

It is a delivery system, not a ready-made drink and not a jar of powder. The flavor stays dry and separate from the water until the moment you want it.

Why a cap?

Powder drinks traditionally need a glass or jug, a spoon, a measure of powder and a stirring step. That suits the kitchen, but not drinking from your own bottle on the move. JUZY puts the flavor right where your bottle already is: water stays in the bottle, flavor travels in the cap.

Inside the cap

A smart cap with a burst of flavor

Tap the markers to explore. The flavor stays sealed in its own compartment, apart from your water, until the moment you press.

Cap body

A food-grade plastic cap designed to fit a standard 28 mm PET bottle neck, with a tamper-evident ring.

Push-release mechanism

A press-down button with a short stem that breaks or opens the seal.

Powder chamber

A small sealed compartment holding one serving of flavor powder.

Moisture-barrier seal

A foil or similar barrier at the base of the chamber, protecting the powder from humidity.

Outer packaging

A printed pouch or sleeve protecting the cap and carrying the brand, flavor, ingredients and instructions.

    Questions

    Good to know

    How do I use JUZY?

    Press the top of the cap to release the powder into your water, shake the bottle for about ten seconds, then enjoy your fruit-flavored drink.

    Is it a ready-made drink?

    No. JUZY is a flavor delivery system built into a bottle cap. Your own water becomes the drink.

    Which bottles will it fit?

    JUZY is being designed for standard 28 mm PET bottle necks. Final compatibility will be confirmed after testing.

    Which flavors are planned?

    Strawberry, mango, orange and grape are planned first. Lemon-mint is under consideration, and local flavors may follow.

    Can I buy it yet?

    JUZY is currently a product concept preview. Pricing and where to buy will be announced on this website and on our social channels.
